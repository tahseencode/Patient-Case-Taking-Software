Privacy Policy & Data Protection

DPDP Act 2023 Compliant ABDM Milestone-M1/M2/M3 AES-256 GCM Encrypted

1. Patient Data Sovereignty & Consent

Medify.AI operates under strict patient-first consent models conforming to India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) framework. No protected health information (PHI) is shared without explicit digital authorization.

2. Zero Unencrypted Storage

All demographic data, chief complaints, SOCRATES symptom logs, and AYUSH Prakriti assessments are stored with client-side and server-side encryption. Clinical notes are accessible strictly by authorized OPD physicians.

3. ABHA Identity & Health Locker Interoperability

When you link your 14-digit ABHA ID, records are encrypted using ABDM-compliant RSA public-key exchange protocols. Patients retain full rights to revoke consent, request record erasure, or transfer their clinical summaries to any ABDM-certified personal health record (PHR) app.

4. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

For any queries regarding medical privacy, data rectification, or grievance redressal, contact our designated Data Protection Officer at: contact@tahseen.co.

Terms of Service & Clinical Disclaimer

Clinical Decision Support (CDSS) Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020

1. Clinical Decision Support Scope

Medify.AI is an AI-powered digital intake, clinical history summarization, and AYUSH triage platform. It assists registered medical practitioners (RMPs) and does NOT replace licensed physician judgment. Final diagnoses, prescriptions, and treatment plans must always be validated by an attending doctor.

2. Emergency Care & Red-Flag Triage Protocol

If a patient presents with severe emergency symptoms (e.g., crushing chest pain, sudden unilateral numbness, severe breathing difficulty, profuse hemorrhaging), our automated triage triggers a Red-Flag alert. In such cases, patients must immediately report to an in-person emergency department (ED/Casualty).

3. Permitted Usage

By accessing Medify.AI, healthcare providers agree to use the software in compliance with the NMC Act, Ayush Code of Ethics, and National Health Authority standards.

Revolutionizing Healthcare Case-Taking

Medify.AI was engineered to solve high OPD wait-times and fragmented patient case-taking across Indian clinical environments. By pairing multilingual speech recognition with clinical SOCRATES dialogue analysis and AYUSH Prakriti profiling, Medify.AI condenses 15-minute intake workflows into under 2 minutes.

Key Platform Capabilities:

  • Multilingual Voice Intake: Real-time voice transcription in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, and English.
  • Standardized SOCRATES History: Systematic mapping of Site, Onset, Character, Radiation, Associations, Time, Exacerbating factors, and Severity.
  • Integrated AYUSH Pariksha: Deep dual assessment of Ayurvedic Prakriti (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) alongside allopathic clinical triage.
  • Native ABDM FHIR R4 Bundle Generator: Automated creation of standard HL7/FHIR Encounter, Condition, and DiagnosticReport bundles.
